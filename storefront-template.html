Our Story

A taste of home in every cup

Selam Café was born from a love of Ethiopian coffee culture. We serve hand-roasted Yirgacheffe and Sidama beans, fresh pastries baked daily, and traditional snacks — all in a warm, welcoming space in the heart of Bole.

Every cup is made with care. Every guest is treated like family.
